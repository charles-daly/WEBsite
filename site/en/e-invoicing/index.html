FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does the September 2026 deadline apply to my company?

For receiving, yes: every VAT-registered company in France must be able to receive electronic invoices from 1 September 2026. Issuing became mandatory on that date for large and mid-sized companies, and applies to SMEs and micro-businesses from 1 September 2027.

Do I need X++ development?

Rarely for the core work. Formats run through Electronic Reporting and exchanges go through the approved platform. Where an integration needs code, I quote it separately with a partner developer.

What does the €4,000 health check include?

Five days of gap assessment against the mandate, a review of your ER formats and platform flows, and a prioritised, costed remediation plan.
